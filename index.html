What if a patch could see beyond the surface?

Your notes stay private. Your note is encrypted in this browser. Only the encrypted file and your email address are sent to Arteria’s email service. Your passphrase is never sent. This demo doesn’t include sample patch readings because they aren’t real health data.

About Arteria

ARTERIA · v1.0.0

A wearable companion research application for the 2026 Congressional App Challenge: acquire, inspect and review measurements with clear provenance.

Arteria is not a clinically validated diagnostic tool. It does not diagnose DVT or calculate a medical risk score. Signal checks describe engineering data quality.

Software development and testing were AI-assisted. Students should review the code and describe their actual contributions.
